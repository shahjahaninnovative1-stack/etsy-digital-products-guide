Questions

How much should I set aside for taxes if I'm self-employed?

It depends on your profit, filing status, state and any W-2 job, so there's no single right percentage. The Seekar Tax App works it out from your own numbers and shows the % to save from each payment.

When are quarterly estimated taxes due?

For tax year 2026: April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. For tax year 2027: April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2027, and January 18, 2028 (January 15 is a Saturday and January 17 is a federal holiday).

What is the IRS mileage rate for 2026?

72.5 cents per business mile, from January 1, 2026 (IRS Notice 2026-10). The IRS usually announces the next year's rate in December.

Do I get a 1099 if a client paid me less than $2,000?

Not necessarily. The 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC threshold is $2,000 for payments made in 2026 and $2,100 for 2027. Payment apps and marketplaces send a 1099-K at $20,000 and 200 transactions. You still report all income.

How is self-employment tax calculated?

12.4% Social Security (up to the wage base: $184,500 for 2026, $192,300 for 2027, reduced by W-2 wages) plus 2.9% Medicare, on 92.35% of your net profit. Half is deductible.

How do I avoid an underpayment penalty?

Pay at least the smaller of 90% of this year's tax or 100% of last year's tax (110% if last year's AGI was over $150,000) through withholding and quarterly payments.

Can I use standard mileage and deduct gas too?

No. For a vehicle you use either the standard mileage rate or your actual costs. The rate already covers fuel and repairs. Parking and tolls are deductible either way.

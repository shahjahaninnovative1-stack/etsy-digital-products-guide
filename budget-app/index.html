Questions

Is there a budget app without a subscription?

Yes. The Seekar Budget App is a one-time purchase on Etsy. There are no monthly fees, and updates are free.

Can I budget without linking my bank account?

Yes. You type your balance (or import a CSV file you download from your bank) and the app does the rest. It never asks for bank logins.

Does it work offline and on my phone?

Yes. Open the link, add it to your home screen on iPhone or Android, and it works without internet after the first visit. There's also a desktop version.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded and there's no account. You can download a backup file any time.

What does safe to spend mean?

Your balance minus every bill and debt payment due before your next payday, minus your savings set-asides and a safety buffer. It's the amount you can spend freely until you're paid again.

Which currencies does it support?

24 currencies, including USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD, NZD, INR and AED.
